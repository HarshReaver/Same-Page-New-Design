About Us

Chai Adda is a tiny café tucked into the lane just outside the college gate. We started in 2019 with one kettle and a few stools, and now we are the place students come to for cutting chai, hot snacks and a quiet corner to finish assignments.

Everything is made fresh each morning, priced to fit a student's pocket.
